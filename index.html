Welcome to GCET

Welcome to GCET, where we empower future technologists and innovators. Our mission is to provide world-class engineering education that drives research, entrepreneurship, and nation building.

  • Global Curriculum: Industry-aligned syllabus with focus on AI, IoT, Cloud, and Full Stack Development.

About Us

Established in 2000, GCET is one of the leading engineering institutions in North India. We are committed to academic excellence and producing engineers who lead with ethics and innovation.

  • Accredited Excellence: NBA and NAAC A+ accredited programs with global recognition.
  • Smart Infrastructure: Digital classrooms, AI labs, and a central library with 1 Lakh+ books and journals.
  • Placement Record: 200+ companies visit yearly. Highest package: 1.5 Cr. Recruiters include Microsoft, Adobe, Goldman Sachs.
